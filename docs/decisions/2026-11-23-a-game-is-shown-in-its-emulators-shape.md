# A game is shown, and pictured, in the shape its emulator declares

- **Decided:** A game is drawn on the TV in the shape its emulator declares, a vertical arcade game too, and every picture the console makes of a game (a screenshot, a save state's, a save's) is made in that same shape: one function says it for both (`cab::shownAspect`, `core.h`). The frame's own pixels give the shape only when the emulator declares none. A picture of a small frame is stretched along one side, so no pixel of it is lost; none is made past 1920x1080 or its frame's own size, whichever is larger.
- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Why:** Most systems' pixels are not square, so a picture of the raw frame does not look like the game: Nintendo 64's came out two thirds too wide, an arcade board's up to 71% too wide, Super Nintendo's and NES's an eighth or more too narrow (the A9's log and its state pictures, 2026-11-23). On the TV only vertical arcade games were off. Their declared shape was set aside on 2026-09-19 because it is turned already, and inverting it, as Cabinet once did, turns it twice; raw pixels took its place on the belief that the boards that turn have square ones. PGM's 448x224, CPS-2's 384x224 and older boards' 256x224 have not: their games were drawn 540, 630 and 945 wide at 1080 lines, where the cabinet's 3:4 screen is 810. FBNeo, MAME 2003-Plus, MAME 0.289 and Flycast, the four that turn, each declare the shape as it is shown (their source at our pins), so it is used as it comes. Turning it back into "whichever of the two is nearer the pixels" was not done: it is a guess, and the four agree. RomM's web player and Argosy send the raw frame of a core that draws on the processor; a picture that looks as the game does was chosen over one that matches theirs.
- **Numbers:** #725, "Pictures sent to RomM have the emulator's pixel shape: a Nintendo 64 one comes out squashed".
- **Replaces:** in docs/PROJECT.md, "The declared aspect of a turned board is ALREADY turned" (2026-09-19), whose rule was raw pixels for a turned picture, and "at the core's resolution, as Cabinet sends" for a state's picture (#99, "Save states: upload a screenshot with each state, as Cabinet does", 2026-09-26).
