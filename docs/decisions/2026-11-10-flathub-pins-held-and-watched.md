# Flathub's pins hold on every console, the graphics driver's too, and are watched monthly

- **Decided:** Every console ends with exactly the Flathub builds `flatpaks.list` names: an app installs without touching its runtime, each pin moves that one build and names its branch, and the whole list is read back at the end and pinned again where something moved. The graphics driver Switch and original Xbox draw with (Flathub's Mesa, `org.freedesktop.Platform.GL.default`, both its 25.08 and 25.08-extra copies) is pinned too, at the builds the A9 runs (Mesa 26.2.2). A check on the 1st of each month, and on any pull request that changes a pin, fails while a pin is gone from Flathub or has fewer than two older builds left behind it.
- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Why:** A tester's console installed on 2026-11-06 ended with both runtimes past their pins while the setup logged every one pinned, then failed to pin one of them every 6 hours, because the newest xemu had brought a second runtime branch; a later update moved Switch's libraries back seven weeks under it. The driver had no pin at all, so on the same image the A9 drew Switch with Mesa 26.2.2 and that console with 26.2.5. Flathub keeps only its last few builds of each (4 to 7), so a pin left alone is eventually gone and new consoles quietly get the newest; the check warns while there is time to try the next build on the A9. The cost: a fix in Flathub's Mesa reaches Switch and original Xbox only when its pin moves.
- **Numbers:** no issue; found in a portable tester's diagnostic report of 2026-11-10. It rides with #688, "The controller list and Steam's spare are checked monthly, with the Bazzite base".
- **Replaces:** none.
