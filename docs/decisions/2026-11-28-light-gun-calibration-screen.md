# The light guns' calibration screen: the shot first, the camera after

- **Decided:** Settings' Light gun calibration row and a gun's D-pad held
  left three seconds open one screen (#575 rule 13), anywhere: in the menus,
  and over a game, which it pauses. It asks for a shot at the middle first,
  then sweeps the camera while the player keeps aiming there, then shows the
  gun's aim. The console opens it from the hold it reads on the gun's own
  keys, and takes the shot as Sinden's calibration only when the gun's aim
  was held at exactly the middle, waiting for Sinden to let go. While Sinden
  holds a gun's aim, only that gun can leave the screen or shoot.
- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Why:** while Sinden's software calibrates it holds the aim at the middle,
  so the camera cannot be judged until the shot ends it; and the shot says
  which of two guns is calibrating and that it points at the middle. The
  hold opens the screen in the menus too, since Sinden's software calibrates
  wherever the hold happens, and an unseen calibration would be set by the
  gun's next shot wherever it pointed. Reading the hold on the console's own
  side works whether or not Sinden's software sees the D-pad while the
  console holds the gun's input devices, which was not known before a gun
  was in hand: the screen's log says whether the aim was held at the
  middle.
  Not over an emulator run as a program yet: none is handed a gun.
- **Numbers:** #575, "Sinden light guns: plug in and play gun games
  (waiting on the guns)".
- **Replaces:** none.
