# One copy of Sinden's software for each gun, in a world of its own

- **Decided:** Each Sinden light gun plugged in gets its own copy of Sinden's software, started when the gun's serial port appears and stopped when it goes (udev, then `cabinetos-sinden@<port>.service`). Each copy runs in a bwrap world holding only that gun: a `/dev` of its own with the gun's serial port and its camera, the camera chosen as the one on the gun's own USB hub, every other Sinden gun and camera hidden from its `/sys`, and every list of devices it could find a gun in fixed as it was at its start, so a gun plugged in later is hidden too; no network, and a user of its own. So every gun is Sinden's gun 1 and sends Sinden's player 1 buttons; the console tells guns apart by their USB port, never by their keys. Its settings are Sinden's file with the camera named, recoil off with the shortcut that switches it on held back, and its log quiet.
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Why:** Sinden's software, run once for two guns, numbers them by serial port order and pairs the Nth port with the Nth camera. On the reference console a replug made it drive one gun twice, and later give each gun the other's camera (#575, the first day). Its settings can name each gun's camera but not its serial port, so one copy cannot be pinned to the guns. One copy per gun is pinned by what it can see: in its world the only gun is its own, the counting comes out right whatever the order, and one gun replugged or dropping off USB restarts only its own copy. Measured the same night with both guns: each copy counted one gun, drove it with its own camera at 66 frames a second, and read its own calibration from it. Rejected: one copy for every gun, its settings rewritten and the copy restarted at each plug, which still trusts its order for the serial ports and stops the other gun at every plug; a container per gun, which needs a container runtime started for each gun, and where Sinden's software, as the container's first process, ignored SIGTERM; the open drivers (sindenrs, sinden-driver), kept as a fallback by the decision of 2026-11-02. On 2026-11-28 a gun that came back on USB after the other gun's copy started was found by that copy, which looks for guns every second, and broke it.
- **Numbers:** #575, "Sinden light guns: plug in and play gun games (waiting on the guns)".
- **Replaces:** none.
