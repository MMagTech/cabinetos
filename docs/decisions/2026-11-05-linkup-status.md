# Status belongs to each account, and an account shows Offline when nobody is there

- **Decided:** A line on top of LinkUp's screen reads "Status: Online", and pressing it "Status: Offline". It belongs to each account, holds on every console that account signs in on (2026-11-12-linkup-one-person-on-every-console.md), stays set, and asks for no PIN. A console also says Offline for its account after 15 minutes with no press, when the screen turns off, when it sleeps, and in Steam, and the account shows Offline when none of its consoles says Online. One press brings it back. Offline is one word, whatever the reason.
- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Why:** A person can use LinkUp without being on show, and nobody can answer a knock on a console nobody is at.
- **Numbers:** #654, "LinkUp: play a game with a friend on another CabinetOS console over the internet"; PR #666, "LinkUp: the console's side, step 3 of #654".
- **Replaces:** the switch named Appear Offline.
